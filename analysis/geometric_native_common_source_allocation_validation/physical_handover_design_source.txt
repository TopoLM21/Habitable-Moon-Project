  varies with angle/depth. Source old constant-dip receiver director is
  different. Friction-free traction -P*n_new cannot equal -P*n_old unless the
  normals coincide. A declared inherited mechanical frame or a genuine
  prestress/stress reconstruction is needed; scalar pressure is insufficient.
- Exact source bank-tip/Jacobian data do not by themselves provide actual
  supported COMMON attachments or a work-preserving map of old beam modes to
  new cold7/HOT6 fields. The active 8 angular classifications are the old
  incoming-donor archived group; these are different material criteria.

  if its prestress is still physically live. If its material leaves the active
  bulk, its force/work must pass to an explicit material subsystem/transition;
  a constant archived-energy receipt has zero restoring force.
- The source has only nodal cumulative-work histories, not a continuous
  spatial work density. Continuous p/c does not supply that missing density.
